# Google Play privacy review — publisher worksheet

Reviewed 2026-11-17 for the Friend or Foe Android application (`com.friendorfoe`). This is a code-based worksheet, not a published privacy policy or a completed Play Console declaration. Verify the final Play AAB and the services it actually calls before submitting. In particular, **“no data collected” is not supported**. Collection includes transmissions by bundled SDKs and may include ephemeral processing; “shared”, exceptions, and purposes require a separate assessment. [Play Data safety definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)

## Decisions before submission

1. **Privacy policy and consent.** The Android settings screen includes a native reader for the [public-hosting copy of the policy](../android/app/src/main/res/raw/privacy_policy.html). Confirm the public organizational contact and actual retention/deletion arrangements, publish this file as an accessible HTML page, and enter the verified URL in Play Console. For sensitive uses people might not expect, show a plain-language explanation *before* permission/access, stating what leaves the device and when; an OS prompt or listing alone is insufficient. Verify the fresh-install flow, decline paths and recovery. [User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311), [prominent disclosure guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11150561)
2. **Transport security.** The store build requires HTTPS for a configured sensor server, including on LAN. Put a TLS endpoint with a certificate trusted by Android in front of a local server, then enter its final HTTPS URL. Badge and backend requests refuse redirects. The sole cleartext exception is 192.168.4.1. Without an active USB connection, the foreground app automatically sends read-only status requests to that local address, including during onboarding. The request carries no scan results or credentials. A reachable device can see the request and the phone’s network address. Badge control commands use USB. The debug-only emulator bridge is outside the Play build. Check the stock-badge control and telemetry flows before answering the Console’s encryption-in-transit question; do not claim universal encryption merely because the backend uses TLS. [Android network security configuration](https://developer.android.com/privacy-and-security/security-config).
3. **Location and foreground services.** The main manifest declares fine/coarse location and a location foreground service, without `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`. `WatchControls` and `NearbyWatchService` support explicit Start, an ongoing notification and Stop. Prepare a reviewer video showing the actual feature, location notice, start/stop, and behavior when minimized. Justify precision for Wi-Fi scanning/calibration and disclose watch-mode use and data destinations. The permission name alone does not settle whether Play treats the behavior as background-equivalent. Check the applicable Play Console declarations at submission. [Foreground service declarations](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13392821), [location policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9799150)
4. **Accounts, ads and distribution.** No end-user account creation flow or advertising integration was identified in the reviewed app source/direct dependencies. A calibration label or endpoint token is not by itself an app account. Confirm backend/web sign-up paths, actual ad placements and the final merged manifest/dependencies before answering account creation, Contains ads and advertising-ID questions. If the app enables account creation, provide both in-app and web deletion paths. Complete the deletion questions even without accounts. All Android builds query Google Play and open the official listing for installation. Verify the behavior with an eligible internal-test account. GitHub is a user-opened source link. [Account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111), [Play update policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16559646)
5. **Operator sign-off.** Save the bundle version, merged permissions and SDK inventory. Exercise fresh install, BLE-only use, Wi-Fi scan, ADS-B/weather/maps, optional calibration, watch, vision, microphone, save/share and a denial of each sensitive permission. Record destinations and data types using controlled test data. Have the publisher confirm identity/contact, recipients and service-provider agreements, purposes, optionality, retention/logging, deletion rights/process and country-specific operations. Reconcile those answers across the actual release, policy, listing, reviewer instructions and Data safety form.
