## Description

Explore the Bluetooth signals near you. Friend or Foe opens on personal recorders, including the Plaud family. Start a scan to see which devices broadcast a recognizable name or service. Tap a result to see the evidence. A match does not tell you whether someone is recording, and a silent device can go undetected.

Search by recorder family, keep everyday accessories collapsed, and group known iBeacons by network. Try the built-in Plaud name example without sharing your location or allowing Bluetooth access.

Have a compatible scanner station? Connect it to see its Wi-Fi findings, including the network name when available and the reason it was flagged. The app identifies the source of each finding: your iPhone or the station.

Outside, explore nearby aircraft on a map, save a flight or browse the offline aircraft and drone guide. Aircraft lookup is optional and uses a rounded search area. Friend or Foe includes light and dark appearances and works without an account.

## App Review notes

- From Welcome, tap **Open Privacy → Supported recorders & name example**. The prefilled example uses the live name-matching rules without a physical recorder. It is identified as an example and does not populate real sightings.
- **More → Aircraft & drone guide** works offline. Search for `B738`. Nearby aircraft lookup, Apple Maps, and station reports use the network only after the corresponding action.
- Local Bluetooth observations require physical hardware that is actively advertising. iBeacon ranging requires a known UUID and location access. iPhone does not scan surrounding Wi-Fi networks. Provide any optional reviewer station separately and only if it is controlled for review.
- Camera, sound and magnetic field tools are in **More**. They process readings on the device and do not save audio or images. No hardware is required for the reference guide.
